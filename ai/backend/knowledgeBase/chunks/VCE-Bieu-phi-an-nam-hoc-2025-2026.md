   The meal fee has been adjusted from the 2024-2025 school year to compensate for increased costs

   caused by consumer price inflation.

    from the joining day.

    Parents fully understand the School does not guarantee your child having a school meal until the meal

    fee has been paid.

       meal fee will be refunded if the official notice is given at least 7 days in advance.

   The refund will only be issued if the total refundable amount is equivalent to at least one week’s meal fee.

   o  Huỷ sử dụng dịch vụ ăn tại Trường: số tiền hoàn sẽ được thanh toán trong vòng 15 ngày kể từ ngày

       Meal plan cancellation: The refund will be issued within 15 working days from the last date of using

       For special cases: The refund will be issued within 30 days from the end of the school year.
